Conversion Notes:
I made him PL13 to show that he is the equal of Batman in terms of combat skills but his shield, allows him a higher defensive value. Also, it has been established that Cap can go toe to toe with guys of much higher PL like Thor, Hulk, Iron Man and others because of his shield. The way i have always seen it, Cap was always better physically than Batman but Bat was always way smarter, though Cap is no dumbo.
I made Cap as the benchmark for many superheroes in the Marvel Universe. In terms of abilities and combat skills, he sets the bar. With his stats, i basically made most of them at 7, which is the established peak human in Mutants & Masterminds.
Though technically not super powers, his extreme abilities are beyond the average human. Also, i made them not innate as it has been established that his abilities can be shut down through some special circumstances
